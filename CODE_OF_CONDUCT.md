# Code of Conduct

The Sayo community expects respectful collaboration regardless of experience, position, or background.

## Expected behavior

- Critique technology and ideas without attacking people.
- Make differing assumptions explicit and ask for clarification.
- Keep explanations and feedback accessible to newcomers.
- Acknowledge mistakes and welcome corrections.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Discrimination, harassment, threats, or disclosure of private information
- Sexualized or insulting language
- Deliberate disruption of discussion

If a problem occurs, do not escalate it in public. Contact the repository maintainers privately. Depending on the
situation, maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
